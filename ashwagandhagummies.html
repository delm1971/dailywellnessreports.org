What is Ashwagandha and How Does It Work?

Ashwagandha (known botanically as *Withania somnifera*) is classified as an adaptogen—a select group of botanical plants that help the human body adapt to, manage, and recover from physical and psychological stressors. Rather than forcing an artificial crash or unnatural energy spike, adaptogens act like an internal thermostat, stabilizing systemic balance when external factors push you to the limit.

Inside the body, active compounds within the root work directly with your hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis. By helping to modulate your baseline cortisol levels—the primary marker released during high-stress moments—ashwagandha can help quiet an overactive mind, ease physical tension, improve focus, and promote better sleep parameters naturally.
